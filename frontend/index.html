Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started.

How does CoLearn work?

expand_more

Simply create an account with your email, verify your login via OTP, and you can instantly create a 'Suite'. Invite friends with a link and start collaborating in real-time.

Can I study with friends in different locations?

expand_more

Absolutely! CoLearn works over the internet. Our LAN mode is an optional optimization for when you are in the same building, but global sync is always active.

Is my data and study material secure?

expand_more

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption for all data transfers. Your suites are private, and access is restricted to people you explicitly invite.

How many documents can I share?

expand_more

Free student accounts include up to 500MB of cloud storage for PDFs and images per suite. You can always delete old files to make room for new ones.
