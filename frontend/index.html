Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CoLearn study suites.

Is CoLearn completely free to use?

Yes, CoLearn is designed for students and study groups with full access to collaborative whiteboards, suites, and Google Drive syncing.

Do all suite members need to connect Google Drive?

Any member can connect their own Google Drive account to share files. Files uploaded to the suite are accessible to all suite members.

How does the whiteboard real-time sync work?

Canvas strokes, geometric shapes, and text blocks synchronize in real-time across all connected peers using WebSockets and Firebase data channels.
