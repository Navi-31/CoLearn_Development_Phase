Problem 4.1: Orthogonal Projection onto Closed Subspaces

[20 Points]

Let V be an inner product space over ℝ with standard inner product ⟨·, ·⟩. For any finite-dimensional closed subspace W = span{u1, u2, ..., uk}, we define the projection operator PW: V → W.

PW(v) = ∑i=1k ⟨v, ui⟩ ||ui||² ui
Prove that ⟨v - PW(v), w⟩ = 0 for all w ∈ W.

Section 4: Projection Theorems & Spectral Proofs

In finite-dimensional inner product spaces, every subspace W induces an orthogonal direct sum decomposition:

V = W ⊕ W⊥

Every vector v ∈ V can be uniquely expressed as v = w + w' where w ∈ W and w' ∈ W⊥.
