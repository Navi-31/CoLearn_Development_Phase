Proposition 3.2: Orthogonal Decomposition in Hilbert Space V = W ⊕ W⊥

Let W be a finite-dimensional closed subspace of an inner product space V. Every vector v ∈ V decomposes uniquely as v = w + w⊥ where w ∈ W and w⊥ ∈ W⊥.

Inner Product Space NODE_01
Vector Space V (dim = n)

Equipped with standard Hermitian inner product ⟨·, ·⟩

layers Closed Subspace
Subspace W = Span{u₁, u₂, ..., uₖ}
dim(W) = k ≤ n
save_as Orthogonal Space
W⊥ = {v ∈ V : ⟨v, w⟩ = 0, ∀w ∈ W}
Projection: P_W + P_W^⊥ = I_V
