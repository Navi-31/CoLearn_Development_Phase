Proposition 3.2 (Orthogonal Projections)

Let \( V \) be an inner product space and \( W \subseteq V \) a finite-dimensional subspace. For any vector \( \mathbf{v} \in V \), the projection \( \mathrm{proj}_W(\mathbf{v}) \) satisfies:

% Orthogonal decomposition formula

\mathbf{v} = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{\langle \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{u}_i \rangle}{\|\mathbf{u}_i\|^2} \mathbf{u}_i + \mathbf{w}^\perp

where \(\mathbf{w}^\perp \in W^\perp \quad \text{and} \quad \langle \mathbf{w}^\perp, \mathbf{u}_j \rangle = 0\)

Documentation & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Details regarding architecture, data privacy, device compatibility, and storage governance.

How is study suite privacy and access control enforced? expand_more
Each study suite is provisioned as an isolated environment protected by ephemeral 5-digit passcodes or institutional single sign-on (SSO) verification. Room hosts maintain administrative authority to grant read-only observer status, revoke entry permissions, or terminate sessions instantly.
Can CoLearn be accessed across multiple devices simultaneously? expand_more
Yes. The platform is engineered with a responsive browser-first architecture. Researchers commonly utilize a tablet with a stylus for high-precision whiteboard calculations while maintaining a parallel desktop session for reading research manuscripts and monitoring text discussion.
How does Google Drive integration handle document permissions? expand_more
CoLearn utilizes OAuth 2.0 scoped access tokens strictly restricted to files explicitly selected by the user. Documents remain hosted on Google's cloud infrastructure; CoLearn renders synchronous views and markup layers without storing copies of proprietary research files on external servers.
Are formulas rendered client-side or server-side? expand_more
Mathematical formulas written in LaTeX format are rendered directly in the client browser utilizing KaTeX. This ensures instantaneous sub-millisecond redraws during active typing and eliminates computational server roundtrips.
